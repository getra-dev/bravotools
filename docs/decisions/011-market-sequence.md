# ADR-011: Market sequence — Baltics lab → PL + Nordics → DACH

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Status:** Accepted

## Decision
LT/Baltics = laboratory (Sivysta pilot, dictionary matures, proof in €).
Poland active via local partner/reseller + pl.json; Nordics passive
self-serve. DACH only with traction (and likely not solo). Global
self-serve funnel (EN landing, Free plan, AI content) live from day one —
zero live-sales hours spent on it.

## Context
40M Poland is the target, 2.8M Lithuania is the lab — sequence, not
choice. Materials dictionary is language-local (cold start per market);
everything else is global. Competitive scan (Jul 2026): Kojo $93M US
office-procurement; ToolWorks AI tool-tracking US; StructShare→Trimble;
niche "SMB tools+materials+logistics, voice-first, EU" still open —
window is real but not eternal.
