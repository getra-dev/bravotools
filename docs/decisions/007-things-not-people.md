# ADR-007: BravoTools manages things and their money, not people

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Status:** Accepted

## Decision
No workforce management: no timesheets, shifts, crew planning, or labor
as a "resource". People appear only as responsibility (holder, assignee,
site_assignments).

## Context
Workforce mgmt is a separate regulated category (labor code, scheduling).
Adding it would recreate the BravoBIM scope-creep pattern. Also sales
benefit: "we are not an employee-surveillance app" lowers field resistance.

## Future
BravoCrew as a separate ecosystem product if demand appears.
