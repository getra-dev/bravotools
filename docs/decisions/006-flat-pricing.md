# ADR-006: Flat per-org pricing, unlimited field users

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Plans: free / pro / business / enterprise (plan_limits table). Field
workers unlimited and free on ALL plans. Limits apply to office roles,
sites, and features (AI, reconciliation, recharging). Safety features
(inspection reminders) never paywalled.

## Context
Per-user pricing punishes full adoption — and full adoption IS the
product's value (every movement recorded). Per-asset (Hilti model)
produces incomplete registries. Price anchor: monthly "SAVED €" receipt
must exceed license.

## Rejected
Per-user, per-asset, cheap-to-win-enterprise ("imkim mažiau, kibinkim
stambius" — cheap price doesn't attract enterprise, only kills margin).
