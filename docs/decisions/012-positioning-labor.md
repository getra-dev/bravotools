# ADR-012: Positioning — labor-shortage relief, not surveillance

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Core story: "your five people do what seven did" — hours given back to
understaffed teams. Monthly receipt shows SAVED € AND freed hours
(computed from activity_log). Reconciliation/catching stays as a feature,
not the identity.

## Context
Labor shortage is existential for buyers; "catching" is episodic. This
framing also removes conflict for rental-company integrations
("Rental Connect" — digital handover protects both sides) and lowers
worker adoption resistance. Vs Hilti: "Hilti tracks Hilti tools. We track
YOUR tools" (mixed fleets are the SMB norm).
